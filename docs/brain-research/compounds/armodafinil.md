## Summary

Armodafinil (Nuvigil) is the longer-lasting **R-enantiomer of modafinil** and a **prescription (Rx) eugeroic** (wakefulness-promoting agent). Its evidence base **parallels modafinil**: it is well supported by randomized controlled trials for **excessive sleepiness in narcolepsy, shift-work disorder, and obstructive sleep apnea (OSA)** (wakefulness graded **3** — solid). Evidence for **enhancing cognition in healthy, non-sleep-deprived adults is modest, limited, and less independently studied than modafinil** — most cognitive gains in trials (memory, attention) were measured in clinically sleepy populations and are plausibly secondary to restored wakefulness. The overall grade is held at **2** because the rigorous, replicated outcomes are wakefulness-in-sleep-disorders, not healthy-cognition enhancement. Pharmacologically it is best characterized as a **weak DAT inhibitor** that raises striatal dopamine (human PET data exist for modafinil; armodafinil is inferred). It is **not a supplement**: it is a Schedule IV controlled substance carrying real risks — Stevens-Johnson syndrome, CYP3A4 induction that **undermines hormonal contraception**, cardiovascular and psychiatric effects, and dependence potential. **Cross-reference the modafinil entry** for shared mechanism and safety class.

## Mechanism

- **Dopamine transporter inhibition (da).** Armodafinil is the R-enantiomer of modafinil, a **weak DAT blocker** that raises extracellular dopamine, especially in the striatum and nucleus accumbens — without the presynaptic dopamine release seen with amphetamines. The direct human evidence (PET DAT occupancy correlating with plasma level and increased extracellular dopamine) is for **modafinil** (Volkow 2009, PMID 19293415); for armodafinil specifically this is **inferred from enantiomer identity**, so the da channel is graded 2 and flagged as assumption.
- **Arousal / wake-promoting circuitry (ne).** Like modafinil, armodafinil promotes cortical arousal through downstream **noradrenergic, orexinergic, and histaminergic** wake-promoting systems rather than a single classical receptor. The precise human target-engagement story for armodafinil alone is largely **inferred from the modafinil literature**.
- **Pharmacokinetic distinction.** Modafinil is a racemate; its S-enantiomer is cleared quickly (~4-5 h) while the R-enantiomer (armodafinil) persists (~13-15 h). Although both products share the same terminal half-life, armodafinil produces **higher late-day plasma concentrations** on a milligram basis and a more monophasic decline (Darwish 2009, PMID 19663523) — the basis of the "longer-lasting" marketing claim.

## Evidence

**Wakefulness in sleep disorders (graded 3 — solid, replicated RCTs):**

- **Narcolepsy.** 12-week, multicenter, double-blind RCT (n=196; 150 mg, 250 mg, or placebo). Armodafinil significantly improved daytime Maintenance of Wakefulness Test (MWT) sleep latency vs placebo (P<0.01) and improved overall clinical condition (CGI-C), memory, attention, and fatigue (Harsh 2006, PMID 16684437).
- **Shift-work disorder.** 12-week RCT (n=254 night-shift workers, 150 mg vs placebo). Armodafinil significantly improved nighttime MWT sleep latency and overall clinical condition, plus measures of long-term memory and attention during night work (Czeisler 2009, PMID 19880686).
- **Obstructive sleep apnea (adjunct to nCPAP).** Pooled analysis of two 12-week double-blind RCTs (n=391 armodafinil at 150/250 mg vs n=260 placebo). Mean MWT latency increased +2.0 min vs −1.5 min placebo (P<0.0001); episodic secondary memory and patient-estimated wakefulness improved and fatigue decreased (Roth 2008, PMID 17874255).

**Healthy-cognition enhancement (graded low — modest/limited):**

- There is **no robust, replicated RCT base** showing armodafinil improves cognition in healthy, well-rested adults. The cognitive improvements seen in the trials above were measured in **clinically sleepy patients** and are most parsimoniously explained by restored alertness rather than a direct pro-cognitive effect.
- Compared with modafinil, armodafinil has been **less independently studied for off-label/healthy enhancement**; the broader modafinil enhancement literature (small, mixed acute effects on executive function under fatigue) is the closest available proxy. Direct controlled enhancement data in healthy non-sleep-deprived adults for armodafinil specifically: **(unsourced)**.

## Safety & interactions

Same safety class as modafinil; cross-reference the modafinil entry.

- **Serious rash (SJS/TEN).** Rare but life-threatening cutaneous reactions, including Stevens-Johnson syndrome and toxic epidermal necrolysis, are reported in post-marketing data for both enantiomers. **Discontinue at the first sign of rash** unless clearly unrelated.
- **Hypersensitivity / DRESS / angioedema.** Multi-organ hypersensitivity and angioedema have been reported; prior hypersensitivity to modafinil contraindicates armodafinil (cross-reactivity).
- **CYP3A4 induction and contraception (major interaction).** Armodafinil is a **moderate CYP3A4/5 inducer**; it reduces plasma levels of CYP3A4 substrates, **including hormonal contraceptives — reducing their efficacy**. Non-hormonal/additional contraception is advised during use and for **1 month after** discontinuation. Cyclosporine, midazolam, and triazolam levels are likewise reduced.
- **CYP2C19 inhibition.** Armodafinil inhibits CYP2C19, potentially raising levels of substrates such as phenytoin, diazepam, propranolol, and omeprazole; warfarin/INR monitoring is prudent.
- **Cardiovascular / psychiatric.** Increased blood pressure and heart rate; caution in cardiac disease. Anxiety, insomnia, agitation, and rare mania/psychosis/suicidal ideation are reported. Headache is the most common adverse event.
- **Dependence / controlled status.** Schedule IV (US) controlled substance with abuse/dependence potential. Not appropriate as a casual or self-dosed nootropic.

## Open questions

- Does armodafinil produce any **direct, durable cognitive benefit in healthy, well-rested adults**, separable from its wakefulness effect? Current trials cannot answer this — endpoints come from sleepy populations.
- Are the human DAT-occupancy and dopamine-increase findings established for **modafinil** quantitatively reproduced by **armodafinil at clinical doses**, or merely assumed from enantiomer identity? (Direct armodafinil PET data: **(unsourced)**.)
- Does the longer late-day plasma profile translate into any cognitive or safety difference (e.g., insomnia, next-day effects) versus equipotent modafinil in healthy users?
- Long-term safety, dependence trajectory, and any tolerance to wake/cognitive effects with chronic off-label use are uncharacterized.
