## Summary

Acetyl-L-Carnitine (ALCAR) is the acetyl ester of L-carnitine. It is dual-purpose: a **mitochondrial fuel cofactor** (the carnitine shuttle moves fatty acids into mitochondria for beta-oxidation, and the acetyl/CoA buffer supports energy metabolism) and an **acetyl donor** that can feed into acetylcholine synthesis. The best human evidence is in **older/impaired populations** — a meta-analysis in MCI/mild Alzheimer's disease and a meta-analysis in depression (skewed toward elderly) — plus peripheral neuropathy. **Evidence in healthy young adults is thin.** Effect sizes in cognition are small. Overall graded **2** (real but modest controlled evidence, confined largely to impaired/aged groups, with a notable harm signal in one chemo-neuropathy setting).

## Mechanism

- **Mitochondrial (mito):** ALCAR donates/accepts acetyl groups via carnitine acetyltransferase, participating in the carnitine shuttle that imports long-chain fatty acids into mitochondria for beta-oxidation, and buffering the acetyl-CoA pool. This supports mitochondrial energy metabolism, the primary and most mechanistically defensible channel. (Pennisi et al., Nutrients 2020 — PMID:32408706)
- **Acetylcholine (ach):** As an acetyl donor, ALCAR can contribute acetyl moieties toward ACh synthesis; reviews describe enhanced cholinergic activity. Plausible but the direct in-vivo human contribution is not well quantified. (PMID:32408706)
- **Neurotrophic (ntrophic):** Neurotrophic / NGF-related effects are described preclinically; **no robust human confirmation** — graded 1 (preclinical-only). (PMID:32408706)

## Evidence

### MCI / mild Alzheimer's disease (meta-analysis)
Montgomery, Thal & Amrein pooled double-blind RCTs of ALCAR vs placebo in MCI and mild AD. Integrated summary effect size **0.201 (95% CI 0.107-0.295)**; Clinical Global Impression of Change ES **0.32 (95% CI 0.18-0.47)**, favoring ALCAR, with benefit by the first 3-month assessment increasing over time. The effect is statistically significant but **small and possibly not clinically noticeable**. (Montgomery et al., Int Clin Psychopharmacol 2003 — PMID:12598816). A later critical update (Pennisi et al., Nutrients 2020 — PMID:32408706) judged dementia evidence inconclusive/contested, citing heterogeneous samples and lack of longitudinal assessment.

### Depression (meta-analysis)
Veronese et al. meta-analyzed RCTs of ALCAR for depressive symptoms (12 RCTs, 791 participants total; mean age ~54, 65% female; placebo-controlled pool 231 vs 216). ALCAR significantly reduced depressive symptoms: **SMD -1.10 (95% CI -1.65 to -0.56), I2 = 86%** (high heterogeneity). Efficacy appeared **greater in older adults**, and vs standard antidepressants ALCAR showed comparable effect with fewer adverse events. The large heterogeneity tempers confidence. (Veronese et al., Psychosom Med 2018 — PMID:29076953)

### Peripheral neuropathy (diabetic, chemo-induced)
ALCAR has supportive signals in painful/diabetic neuropathy, but the most rigorous chemo-neuropathy data is **negative/harmful**: in a phase III placebo-controlled prevention trial in women on adjuvant taxane therapy (3000 mg/day), ALCAR **worsened** chemotherapy-induced peripheral neuropathy and reduced functional status vs placebo at 24 weeks. (Hershman et al., J Clin Oncol 2013 — PMID:23733756)

### Healthy young cognition
Sparse and not supported by RCT-grade data here. **(unsourced)** for any healthy-young nootropic claim.

## Safety & interactions

- Generally well tolerated in cognitive/mood trials; common AEs are GI (nausea), restlessness/agitation, occasional insomnia if dosed late, rare fishy body odor.
- **Harm signal:** worsened neuropathy/function in the taxane CIPN prevention trial — do not assume benign in that context (PMID:23733756).
- Theoretical interactions: thyroid hormone (possible attenuation), anticoagulants (carnitine/INR), and additive cholinergic load. Pregnancy/lactation data insufficient. (interaction specifics: unsourced beyond standard reference cautions)

## Open questions

- Does ALCAR benefit **healthy young** cognition at all, or is the effect confined to deficiency/aging/impairment? (currently unsupported)
- How much of the cognitive signal is mediated by mitochondrial energetics vs cholinergic vs neurotrophic mechanisms in humans?
- Why does ALCAR worsen taxane CIPN despite preclinical/phase-II promise — a cautionary mechanistic puzzle.
- Are depression effects real given I2 = 86%, or driven by small heterogeneous elderly trials?
