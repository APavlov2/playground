## Summary

Coluracetam (BCI-540, MKC-231) is a research-grade, investigational, unapproved racetam-class compound originally developed by Mitsubishi Tanabe Pharma as a high-affinity choline uptake (HACU) enhancer. It is **not** an approved drug for any indication.

Human evidence is **very limited**. It failed to advance in an Alzheimer's disease program, was later in-licensed by BrainCells Inc. and studied in a small Phase IIa trial in major depressive disorder with comorbid anxiety, and did not progress further. There is **essentially no healthy-adult cognitive RCT evidence**; the body of support is predominantly preclinical (rodent cholinergic-lesion and cell-culture models). Overall evidence grade: **LOW (1)**.

## Mechanism

The defining, mechanistically characterized action is enhancement of **high-affinity choline uptake (HACU)** — the rate-limiting step of acetylcholine (ACh) synthesis — via the choline transporter CHT1. In preclinical work, MKC-231 increased HACU and ACh synthesis/release in cholinergically lesioned animals (PMID:18446264), and the mode-of-action study reported that it directly affects CHT1 trafficking and increases the number of transporters working for HACU at the synaptic membrane, with increases in Vmax of HACU and Bmax of [3H]-HC-3 binding (PMID:18461273).

A secondary AMPA-receptor potentiation mechanism has been described in industry/secondary literature in the context of the depression program, but a primary peer-reviewed source for AMPA potentiation in humans was not verified here — treat AMPA potentiation as **(unsourced)** at the level of verified primary evidence in this entry.

## Evidence

**Discontinued Alzheimer's program.** Coluracetam was initially developed and tested for Alzheimer's disease and did not advance successfully through clinical development for that indication (development history; not a single PMID — see open questions). No positive pivotal human cognition data exist. **(human efficacy in AD: not demonstrated / unsourced beyond development-history reporting)**

**MDD-with-anxiety Phase IIa.** After in-licensing by BrainCells Inc., coluracetam was evaluated in a small Phase IIa study in patients with major depressive disorder and comorbid anxiety (reported dosing 80 mg three times daily for 6 weeks). Reported results were modest and the program did not advance. Primary peer-reviewed publication of this trial was **not located/verified** in this search; the trial details are from secondary/company reporting and should be treated as **(unsourced)** at the level of a verified primary PMID.

**Preclinical HACU / cognition (verified primary sources):**
- Mouse working-memory model: MKC-231 improved working-memory deficits and reversed hippocampal ACh depletion induced by AF64A (PMID:7710736).
- Rat water-maze learning: MKC-231 (1–10 mg/kg) improved learning deficits without the autonomic side effects seen with tacrine (PMID:8740080).
- AF64A rat series (HACU/ACh): long-lasting cognitive improvement after repeated dosing (PMID:18461272); enhancement of ACh synthesis and release via HACU (PMID:18446264); CHT1-trafficking mode of action (PMID:18461273).
- Neuroprotection in vitro: protected cultured cortical neurons against glutamate cytotoxicity, attributed to suppression of NO formation downstream of calcium influx (PMID:9541286).
- Schizophrenia-relevant model: reversed phencyclidine-induced behavioral deficits and loss of septal cholinergic neurons in rats (PMID:17467960).

**Gaps:** No verified healthy-adult cognitive RCT. **(healthy-adult nootropic efficacy: unsourced)** No verified human PK (half-life, onset). **(human PK: unsourced)**

## Safety & interactions

Safety data are **sparse**. There is no robust human safety characterization in healthy adults; the human exposure base is limited to the discontinued AD program and the small MDD/anxiety study. Because the primary mechanism is pro-cholinergic (HACU enhancement), additive effects with other cholinergic agents (AChE inhibitors, choline donors) are a **theoretical, mechanism-based interaction** rather than a documented one. Consumer research-grade material has unverified purity and dosing. Treat contraindications and drug interactions as **largely unknown / unsourced**, and regard the compound as investigational and unapproved.
